Rules of infectionEpoch —

Patient zero seeds the pool

The dev wallet launches $PLAGUE and seeds the Meteora pool. Every infection on the map traces back to that one wallet.

Two ways to catch it

Buy on Meteora and the pool infects you. Receive at least 1,000 $PLAGUE from an infected wallet and that wallet becomes your infector. Your first infection is permanent, so your place in the lineage never changes.

Spread it to score

Your epoch score is every wallet downstream of you that was infected this epoch and still holds at least 1,000 $PLAGUE. Deep chains beat one big send.

Take the vaccine

Burn 25,000 $PLAGUE in total to become vaccinated. You stop spreading, your score freezes, you can never be reinfected, and you get a share of the cure fund for the epoch.
